How to use this guide
  1. ▶ START reads every card out loud, in order.
  2. ▶ on any card reads just that card.
  3. The bar at the bottom: ⏪ Rewind goes back one card, ⏸ Pause stops for a moment, ⏹ Stop ends reading, ⏩ Forward skips ahead.
  4. 1× on the bar changes the reading speed.
  5. Keep the screen on while it reads. Phones stop the voice when the screen locks.
  6. Your checklist ticks stay on this phone only.

This guide is not made by Clubhouse. It uses Clubhouse's own help pages and release notes, plus a few news and how-to sites, all listed under . Clubhouse often tests features with only some users, so your app may look a little different. Last checked: October 18, 2026.
